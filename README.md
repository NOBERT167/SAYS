About the Project
The St. Anthony Youth Service (SAYS) website serves as an online platform to share information, news, and resources for the youth of our diocese. This platform enables our community to stay connected and informed about upcoming events, historical information, and other vital details related to the organization.

Features
Home Page: Overview and welcome section.
About SAYS: A section highlighting our Bio, History, and our Patron Saint, St. Anthony of Padua.
Youth Apostolate: Showcases departments within our youth service.
Deaneries/Parishes: Lists all deaneries, each with its specific parishes.
Education and Family Life: Information and resources on our educational and family life initiatives.
News/Events: Latest news and event announcements.
Contact Information: Get in touch with the SAYS organization.
External Links: Useful resources and additional reading.

Usage
The website is structured to provide easy access to information and allow visitors to navigate through the organization's sections. Interactive buttons allow users to expand or collapse content under the "About SAYS" and "Youth Apostolate" sections to display detailed information on each item.
